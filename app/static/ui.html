What this is

Six anchor renderers, ONE intelligence engine. The same data renders many ways — defensive for the board, signal- dense for the CEO, collaborative for sales, thesis-driven as a whitepaper, comparative as a buyer's guide, problem→ solution as a solution guide. Thin patterns surface as "watching"; untagged volume is funnel backdrop only, never quoted as marketing action. Future periods produce a short honest readout naming the period and surfacing current memory as a reference baseline — anchors describe what HAS happened or what IS supported, never what might.
