90-Day Optimization Roadmap
Days 1–14

Critical Technical Fixes

Resolve redirect chains, fix broken links, submit sitemap to Google Search Console, configure canonical tags, fix mobile viewport issues. Estimated: +8–12 ranking positions on crawled pages.

Days 15–30

Content & Metadata Optimization

Rewrite all 22 missing meta descriptions, fix 9 duplicate H1s, optimize 14 thin content pages with 600–900 word rewrites. Add schema markup to homepage, product pages, and blog.

Days 31–60

Core Web Vitals & Page Speed

Compress and lazy-load images across 8 failing pages, defer non-critical JavaScript, implement server-side rendering for JS-heavy product pages. Target: LCP < 2.5s, CLS < 0.10.

Days 61–90

AI Readiness & Keyword Gap

Add FAQ schema and conversational content blocks targeting 80+ keyword gap opportunities. Submit structured data to Bing, DuckDuckGo, and emerging AI search indexes. Begin link outreach targeting DA 40+ legal tech publications.
